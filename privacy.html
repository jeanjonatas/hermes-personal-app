Privacy Policy

Hermes Kandon is a personal, single-user application. It accesses Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts) only on behalf of the account owner, only to perform tasks the owner requests.

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer.
  • No Google user data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is not stored on any server operated by the developer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Last updated: October 2026.
